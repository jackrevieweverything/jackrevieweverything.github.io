# About Everything Review

This is my corner of the internet for reviewing pretty much anything.

Games, gadgets, movies, TV shows, software, trips, clothes, food, places, sports, music, books — if I spend time with it, buy it, watch it, play it, visit it, or otherwise have something useful to say about it, it can end up here.

The reviews are meant to be personal and practical rather than pretending there is a universal score for everything.

I will separate:

- My experience — what I actually tried, watched, used, ate, or visited.
- Facts — specifications, prices, release information, and other details that can be checked.
- Research — information from manufacturers, publishers, developers, official sites, and other sources when context is useful.

If something was great for me but probably not for everyone, I'll say that. If something disappointed me, I'll say that too.

The goal is simple: write down what the experience was actually like.
