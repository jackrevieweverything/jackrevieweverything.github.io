Welcome to **Everything Review**.

This blog is for the stuff that normally ends up as a quick thought, a message to a friend, or a forgotten browser tab.

I'll use it to write about things I actually spend time with: games, gadgets, movies, TV, software, travel, fashion, food, places, sports, music, books, and whatever else turns out to be interesting.

## What to expect

These are personal reviews, so the important question isn't whether something is objectively "the best". I'm more interested in **what the experience was like** and whether it was worth my time, money, or attention.

I'll try to keep the reviews useful by separating my own experience from specifications and outside research.

There won't be a forced score at the end of every post. Sometimes the answer will be "I loved it", sometimes "not for me", and sometimes "I'd wait for a sale".

More reviews will go here as I have something worth writing about.
